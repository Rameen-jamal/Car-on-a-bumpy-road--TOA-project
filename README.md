# Car-on-a-bumpy-road--TOA-project
Paper Automata Project: Car on a Bumpy Road
Project Overview
Our "Car on a Bumpy Road" paper automata is a delightful mechanical paper model that demonstrates fundamental principles of motion transformation. When the user rotates the circular wheels, the carefully designed cam mechanisms convert rotational movement into the up-and-down motion of a car traveling over bumpy terrain. This interactive paper craft brings mechanical engineering concepts to life through simple yet fascinating movements.
The model features a hand-crafted design where circular movement is translated into vertical displacement, creating the illusion of a vehicle navigating through an uneven landscape. Each turn of the wheel creates a sequence of movements that simulate the bouncing and jostling of a car on a rough road.
